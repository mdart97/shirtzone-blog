# Embroidered Jackets for Medical Staff: How to Order for Winter

If your front desk team is wearing mismatched cardigans by December, now is the time to fix it. Embroidered jackets for medical staff are one of the most useful things a clinic, dental office, vet practice, or home health agency can order this fall, and October is the right month to do it. Exam rooms run cold, and a matching embroidered layer looks far more professional than whatever sweater people grabbed on the way out.

Here's how to order them without guesswork.

## Pick the right jacket for the job

Not every jacket works in a clinical setting. The three styles we see practices reorder most:

- **Scrub jackets / warm-up jackets** — snap or zip front, cuffed sleeves, sit right over scrubs. Best for clinical staff who need to move.
- **Lightweight soft shells** — wind-resistant, clean-looking, great for home health and front-office staff who step outside often.
- **Midweight fleece** — the warmest option, ideal for reception and admin teams in drafty front offices.

Many practices split the order: scrub jackets for clinical staff, soft shells for everyone else, same logo on both.

## Why embroidery (and not a print)

Medical outerwear gets washed constantly, often hot. Embroidery holds up to that far better than anything printed, and on a jacket it reads as polished and permanent. Standard placement is a left-chest logo, about 3.5 to 4 inches wide. Adding a name or title under the logo ("Dr. Patel" or "RDH") helps patients know who they're talking to — just plan the name list before you request a quote.

Embroidery has a **6 piece minimum**, so even a small practice with a handful of staff qualifies.

## Your ordering timeline

Embroidery schedules fill up fast once November hits and holiday orders pile in. Work backward:

1. **This week:** collect sizes. Have staff try on a sample size run if you can — jacket fits vary more than scrubs.
2. **Week 2:** send your logo and approve the free digital proof. If your logo is new to us, it needs to be digitized for embroidery; that's a one-time set-up.
3. **Weeks 3–4:** production and shipping. ShirtZone ships nationwide, so the timeline works wherever your practice is.

Ordered by mid-October, jackets land well before the first real cold snap — and they double as a staff appreciation gift if you hand them out in December. (We covered more holiday gifting ideas in [Custom Holiday Gifts for Employees](/p/custom-holiday-gifts-for-employees/).)

Healthcare teams turn over, and the worst outcome is a new hire in a different jacket for six months.
