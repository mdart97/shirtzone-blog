# When to Order Fall Sports Jerseys (Before It's Too Late)

Football, soccer, volleyball, and cross country all kick off within the next month, and if your team's jerseys aren't ordered yet, you're closer to a scheduling problem than you think. The question we hear most this time of year — when do I actually need to order fall sports jerseys — has a real answer, and it's earlier than most coaches and team parents expect.

## The Order Window You're In Right Now

Custom jerseys aren't a same-week product. Between finalizing the roster, picking sizes, approving art, and running production, a realistic order needs **3 to 5 weeks** from "let's do this" to boxes in hand — more if you're adding embroidered logos on top of everything else. If your first game or scrimmage is in early September, the order needs to go in now, not after Labor Day.

## What Actually Slows an Order Down

It's rarely the printing itself. It's waiting on a final roster, chasing down sizes from twenty different parents, or deciding on art after the deadline has already passed. Lock in three things early and the rest moves fast: final player count (with a couple extras for late signups), a size chart collected in one place, and approved artwork before you request a quote. Teams that show up with those three things ready can go from quote to production the same week.

## Screen Printing vs. Vinyl: Getting Logos and Numbers Right

Team logos, mascots, and sponsor names on the back go on with **bulk screen printing** (12 piece minimum) — it holds up to a full season of practices and washes, and once the design exists, reordering the same jersey for a new player later is fast since the screens are already made. Player names and numbers are a different job entirely: those go on with **heat-pressed vinyl**, which is the only way we do names and numbers. It lets us personalize each jersey individually without cutting a new screen for every number on the roster — numbers are never screen printed. Want matching sideline gear or embroidered coach polos too? That's a separate order worth planning alongside the jerseys, not instead of them.

## A Simple Timeline to Follow

Four weeks out: finalize your roster and collect sizes. Three weeks out: get your quote and approve art. Two weeks out: production and personalization. One week out: jerseys in hand with time to spare before the opening whistle. Running behind that? Tell us your actual game date when you request a quote — we'll tell you straight whether it's doable and what to trim if it's tight.
