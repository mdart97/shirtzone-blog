# Custom Holiday Gifts for Employees: Order Early This Year

Custom holiday gifts for employees are one of those projects that always seems to sneak up on people. Then December hits, embroidery calendars are packed solid, and the options left are whatever's fastest — not whatever's best. If you manage a team and want something staff will actually keep, late August is exactly when to start planning.

## Why the lead time matters more than you think

Embroidery digitizing, garment sourcing, and production all take real time, and every other business in town is placing the same kind of order between Thanksgiving and Christmas. Shops (ours included) fill up fast in that window. Getting your design approved and your order in now means you get your first pick of jacket styles and cap colors instead of whatever's still in stock in mid-December.

## What actually works as a gift

Skip the printed logo tee — nobody's excited to unwrap a t-shirt. Two decoration methods consistently perform better as gifts because they read as quality, not swag:

- **Embroidered jackets, quarter-zips, and beanies.** Embroidery holds up wash after wash and looks like a real garment detail instead of a giveaway. Minimum order is 6 pieces, so even a small office can do this properly.
- **Leather-patch caps.** A debossed or laser-etched leather patch on a structured cap feels more like a nice gift and less like a marketing handout. Minimum is 12 pieces, which fits most teams and departments easily.

Both methods work on the jackets, quarter-zips, beanies, and caps people will actually wear outside of work — which is the whole point of a gift versus a uniform.

## The part most businesses don't know about

If you've ordered from us before — for staff shirts, event gear, anything — and you want to reuse that same logo on this year's gift, there's no setup fee for an exact reorder. The art's already on file. You only pay a setup fee when it's genuinely new artwork: a new digitized logo, a new patch design, something we haven't produced for you before. First-time holiday order this year? Expect a one-time setup charge for the embroidery digitizing or patch tooling — after that, it's on file for good.

## A simple timeline that avoids the December scramble

- **Now through early September:** Finalize who's getting gifts and pick embroidery vs. leather-patch caps (or both)
- **Mid-September:** Approve your digital proof and lock sizes
- **October:** Production and quality check, done well before the holiday rush
- **November:** Gifts in hand, no last-minute stress

Waiting until Thanksgiving week to start this project is how businesses end up settling. Starting now is how they end up with something employees actually wear into January.
