## Why Local Businesses Are Switching to Leather Patch Caps

A screen-printed logo on a t-shirt says "staff uniform." A leather patch on a structured cap says "brand." That's the whole appeal — the patch sits raised and tactile on the front panel, works on any cap color, and doesn't crack, fade, or peel the way a printed logo can after a season of sun and washing. For a Redding business handing hats to clients, vendors, or new hires, that durability matters: it's still representing you a year from now.

Coffee shops, breweries, real estate offices, and retail shops around Shasta County are the ones ordering these most. A patch cap works as a $15 add-on gift with a purchase, a staff perk that doubles as advertising, or a giveaway at a local event — all without looking like a walking billboard.

Leather patch caps have a **12 piece minimum** per order. Setup covers digitizing your logo into a patch die — that's a one-time cost for new artwork. Reorder the exact same patch next year, or the year after, and there's no setup or re-set fee at all, because your art stays on file. We send a free digital proof before anything gets made, so you see exactly how the patch sits on the cap before committing.
