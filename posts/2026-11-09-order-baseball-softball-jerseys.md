# When to Order Baseball and Softball Jerseys for Spring

If you're wondering when to order baseball and softball jerseys for spring, the honest answer is: earlier than you think. Most leagues open practice in February and play opening day in March, but the ordering window closes well before that. Between holiday shop closures, January sign-up chaos, and every other league placing orders at the same time, the coaches and league boards who plan in November and December are the ones who hand out uniforms at the first practice instead of the third game.

Here's a timeline that works, plus the decisions to make now.

## The spring jersey timeline

- **November–December:** Lock your design, colors, and budget. League boards usually approve spending around now, so get a quote while you can still adjust.
- **Early January:** Approve your free digital proof. Order blank jerseys for your expected roster plus a few extras.
- **Mid-to-late January:** Final rosters come in. Send names, numbers, and sizes.
- **Early February:** Jerseys delivered, ready for practice and team photos.

Waiting until rosters are final to *start* the process is the most common mistake. The design and proof stage can happen months ahead — only the names and numbers have to wait.

## Split the order: design now, numbers later

A typical baseball or softball jersey has two parts, and they're handled differently:

1. **The team logo or wordmark on the front** is screen printed. That's the part with set-up, so it's the part to get approved early.
2. **Player names and numbers** are heat-pressed vinyl. That's the only way we apply names and numbers, and it means each jersey can be personalized once you know who's on the team.

Screen printing has a 12 piece minimum. Most rosters run 12–15 players, but if yours is smaller, round up with coaches' shirts, a couple of blank-numbered spares for late sign-ups, or matching tees for the dugout parents.

## Don't forget the caps

A baseball uniform isn't done without a cap. Embroidered team caps have a 6 piece minimum, so even a small travel squad can get structured caps with a clean front logo. For coaches and league volunteers, a leather patch cap (12 piece minimum) is a sharp upgrade that reads "staff" without looking like the player uniform.

## Plan for mid-season adds

Someone always joins in week three. Order two or three extra jerseys in common sizes (youth L and adult S cover a lot of rosters), and keep track of which numbers are open. If you need more later, an exact reorder of the same design is never charged set-up fees — your art stays on file, so adding players is quick and cheap.
