# Embroidered Polos for a Dental Office: Order Now for January

If you're shopping for embroidered polos for a dental office, late October is the right moment to start. A lot of practices want a fresh, matching look for the new year — new hires starting in January, a rebrand, or just retiring the faded shirts from three years ago. But November and December are the busiest months for embroidery, with holiday gift orders filling the schedule. Get your order in now and your team walks in on January 2nd looking sharp.

## Why polos (and why embroidered)

Scrubs handle the operatory. Polos cover everyone else — front desk, treatment coordinators, office managers — and they're what the whole team wears to community events, school dental days, and lunch-and-learns. A polo with an embroidered logo reads as professional in a way a printed tee doesn't.

Embroidery also survives what a dental office throws at it: frequent hot washes, the occasional disinfectant splash, and years of wear. The stitched logo won't crack or fade, so the shirt looks the same in year three as it did on day one. (If your clinical team also needs embroidered scrubs, we covered that in [embroidered scrubs for a small clinic](/p/embroidered-scrubs-clinic/).)

## Picking the right polo

A few things matter more than brand names:

- **Performance fabric vs. cotton blend.** Moisture-wicking polyester stays crisp and resists stains — great for staff on their feet all day. Cotton blends feel softer and suit front-office roles.
- **Ladies' and men's cuts.** Offer both. A properly fitted women's polo makes a real difference for staff who wear it every day.
- **Color.** Navy, black, charcoal, and deep teal hide wear well and look clean with white or tone-on-tone thread. Avoid light colors for anyone near the chair.

## Logo placement and sizing

Left chest is the standard — roughly 3.5 to 4 inches wide. Want to add a name or title? A single line of text under the logo, or on the right chest, works well. Keep fine details simple: very thin lines and tiny text don't stitch cleanly at chest size, so we'll suggest small tweaks if your logo needs them. You'll get a free digital proof before anything is stitched.

## Quantities, minimums, and set-up

Embroidery has a **6 piece minimum**, so even a small practice qualifies. A smart rule: order **2–3 polos per staff member** so nobody is wearing the same shirt every day, plus a couple of spares in common sizes for the next new hire.

There's a one-time set-up fee to digitize new artwork. After that, your logo stays on file — and **exact reorders are never charged set-up or re-set fees**. When you hire in March, you order two more polos and pay only for the shirts.
