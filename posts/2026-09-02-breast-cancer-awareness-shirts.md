## Why the timing matters more than you think

October is the single biggest month of the year for awareness-shirt orders. Every clinic, dental office, and home health group in the country wants pink polos, pink scrub tops, or pink team tees on staff at the same time. Shops fill their production calendars fast. If you're ordering for a Redding clinic and want shirts in hand by October 1st, get your order placed by mid-September — that gives enough room for proofing, printing, and any reprints if a size runs short.

## What actually works for staff awareness shirts

For most practices, a simple screen-printed design beats anything elaborate — a pink ribbon, your practice name, maybe a short phrase like "Early Detection Saves Lives." Screen printing is the right call here because you're outfitting a full staff roster (our screen printing minimum is 12 pieces), the design is usually one or two colors, and you want a low per-shirt cost since staff often just wear these over regular clothes for the month, not as a uniform replacement.

If your practice already does embroidered polos as a daily staff uniform (see our [guide to embroidered scrubs for clinics](/p/embroidered-scrubs-clinic/)), you don't need to switch methods just for October — a screen-printed pink tee layered over normal workwear, or worn on casual Fridays through the month, gets the same visibility without redoing your whole uniform order.

## The part practices forget: this repeats every year

Awareness shirts are one of the easiest reorders in the business, but only if the art stays on file. Order this year's design once, and next October you can reorder the exact same shirts with **no set-up fee** — the screens are already made, so you're only paying for print and garments. That's the same reorder logic we talk about in our piece on [ordering holiday gifts early](/p/custom-holiday-gifts-for-employees/): the first order costs more because of new art; every exact reorder after that is just production cost. If you want to tweak the wording or add a new team member's name next year, that's a small update, not a from-scratch design fee.
