# Custom Staff Shirts for Redding Businesses: Plan a January Refresh

If you run a shop, restaurant, salon, or service business in Redding, mid-November is the right time to order custom staff shirts for the new year. The holiday rush fills production calendars fast, and January brings new hires, fresh budgets, and a natural moment to retire the faded, mismatched shirts your team has been wearing since spring. Order now and your crew walks in on the first Monday of January looking like one business.

Here's how to plan it so you buy once and reorder easily all year.

## Count your team, then add a cushion

Start with headcount, then plan for turnover. A simple formula that works for most small businesses:

- **3 shirts per full-time employee**, 2 per part-timer, so nobody wears the same one every shift
- **Add 15–20% extra** in your most common sizes for new hires through spring

A team of 10 full-timers and 6 part-timers lands around 42 shirts, plus 6–8 extras. That clears the 12-piece screen printing minimum easily and puts you into better price breaks. If you're only outfitting a few managers, embroidery starts at just 6 pieces.

## Pick the decoration method by job, not by habit

- **Screen printing** is the value play for tees and crewnecks your staff works hard in: retail floors, kitchens, warehouses, detail shops. A bold front-chest logo with a big back print turns every employee into a walking sign.
- **Embroidery** reads more polished on polos, button-ups, and quarter-zips for front-desk, sales, and client-facing roles. It also survives commercial washing for years.
- **Leather-patch caps** are a sharp add-on for outdoor and counter staff, and they make a good "welcome to the team" piece. Minimum is 12.

Many businesses split it: screen-printed tees for the whole crew, embroidered polos for leads and managers. Our [decoration method guide](/p/choosing-your-decoration-method/) walks through the tradeoffs in more detail.

## Choose a garment you can get again

Uniform programs fall apart when the shirt you picked gets discontinued in July. Stick with core colors (black, navy, charcoal, heather gray) from established brands, and avoid trendy seasonal shades. We'll steer you toward styles that stay in stock year-round.

## Set it up once, reorder without set-up fees

This is the part most owners don't realize. Set-up fees only apply to **new artwork**: new screens or new embroidery digitizing. Once your logo is on file, an **exact reorder never carries a set-up or re-set fee**. When you hire three people in April, you order their shirts in the same design and skip that cost entirely.

That's why it pays to get the January design right. Lock in the logo, placement, and ink or thread colors now, and every reorder after that is simpler and cheaper. For more ways to stretch the budget, see [the bulk order playbook](/p/bulk-order-playbook/).
