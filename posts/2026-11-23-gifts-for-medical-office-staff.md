# Gifts for Medical Office Staff That Actually Get Worn

If you're hunting for gifts for medical office staff this year, skip the gift cards and the coffee mugs that end up in the break room cabinet. The gift that gets used every week is apparel with your practice's logo on it: something warm, comfortable, and nice enough that your front desk, MAs, and hygienists reach for it on cold mornings. The catch is timing. It's late November, and embroidery schedules fill up fast in December. Order this week or next and you'll have gifts in hand before the holiday party.

## The best gift pieces for clinic and office teams

These are the items healthcare teams actually wear, not just keep:

- **Embroidered quarter-zip pullovers.** Professional enough for the front desk, warm over scrubs, and the left-chest logo looks sharp. This is the one we recommend most.
- **Embroidered fleece or soft-shell vests.** Great for staff who run warm or move between exam rooms all day, with no sleeves getting in the way.
- **Embroidered beanies.** A low-cost add-on for every team member, including part-timers and per-diem staff.
- **Leather-patch caps.** A premium-feeling piece for the doctors, office manager, or the whole team if your crew likes hats. 12 piece minimum.
- **Screen-printed crewneck sweatshirts.** If you want a bigger, bolder design, like a team name or a "Est." date with your logo, screen printing gives you that at a lower per-piece price. 12 piece minimum.

Embroidery starts at just 6 pieces, so even a small practice can gift every staff member a quality piece without buying a pile of extras.

## Getting sizes without spoiling the surprise

Order by role, not by guess. Most offices land on something close to 1 S, 3 M, 3 L, 2 XL per ten people, but ask your office manager. They usually know. For unisex pullovers, offer one size up for anyone between sizes. Vests and beanies are forgiving if you're unsure.
