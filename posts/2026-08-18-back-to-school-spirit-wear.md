# Back-to-School Spirit Wear: What Redding PTAs Should Order Now

Back-to-school spirit wear only works if it's in kids' hands before the first pep rally, and mid-August is the window for Redding-area PTAs and booster clubs to get orders moving. School starts in the next couple of weeks, homecoming and fall festivals follow within a month or two after that, and spirit wear takes real production time — not something you can decide on the Monday before you need it.

## Why the Order Window Is Right Now

Between picking a design, collecting sizes from families, and running production, a realistic spirit wear order needs 3 to 4 weeks from "let's do this" to shirts in hand — longer if a booster club is also adding embroidered logos for staff or coaches on top of the student run. Order now and you've got shirts ready for the first home game, back-to-school night, and homecoming without a scramble in September.

## What Actually Counts as Spirit Wear

Spirit wear covers a lot: club shirts, grade-level tees, teacher and staff shirts, booster club gear, and the shirt every PTA sells at back-to-school night to raise a little extra cash. All of it goes on with **bulk screen printing**, which has a 12 piece minimum per design — easy to clear once you're printing for a whole grade or club roster. One screen setup covers every size from youth small to adult XXL, so a single order can outfit kindergartners and teachers in the same design without extra cost per size.

## Sizing a Whole School Without Guessing

The same pre-order approach that works for fundraiser shirts works here: collect sizes and orders for two weeks through a simple form, then print exactly what sold plus a small cushion in the popular youth mediums and adult larges. It beats guessing quantities and getting stuck with a box of unsold 2XL when what you needed was more youth mediums. If your booster club is also running an actual fundraiser tee alongside spirit wear, [our design tips for fundraiser shirts](/p/fundraiser-tees/) cover pricing and pre-order timing in more detail.

## Reordering Next Semester Costs Less Than You'd Think

Once this fall's spirit wear design is printed, the screens stay on file with us. Winter spirit week or a spring reorder of the same design skips the setup fee entirely — you're only paying for the shirts, not to remake the art. Only a genuine design change, like adding a new grade name or updated mascot, would count as new art and carry a setup fee. It's worth designing something you'd want to reuse all year, not just for the first week of school.
